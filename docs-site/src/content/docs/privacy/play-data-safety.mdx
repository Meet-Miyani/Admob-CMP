This guide outlines the data collection practices of the Google Mobile Ads (GMA) SDK on Android to assist you when completing the Google Play Data safety declaration in the Play Console.

> [!NOTE]
> This documentation is provided for informational purposes and does not constitute legal advice. Data safety requirements depend on your full application architecture, including mediation adapters, third-party analytics, backend logging, and custom app logic. Always refer to Google's official [Google Play Data Disclosure documentation for GMA Next-Gen](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/next-gen/privacy/play-data-disclosure).

## Overview and SDK scope

The `admob-cmp` library acts as a Kotlin Multiplatform wrapper for the Google Mobile Ads SDK. While `admob-cmp` itself only requests the `INTERNET` permission, the underlying Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects specific data points required for ad serving, analytics, and fraud prevention.

When completing your Google Play Data safety declaration, you must account for all data collected by your application, including data collected transitively by SDK dependencies.

## Automatically collected data categories

According to Google's current GMA Next-Gen data disclosure guidance, the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects the following data categories by default:

| Data category | Stated purposes |
|---|---|
| **Device or other IDs** | Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention, security, and compliance |
| **IP address** | Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention, security, and compliance |
| **User product interactions** | Advertising or marketing, Analytics |
| **Diagnostic information** | Analytics, Fraud prevention, security, and compliance |

Google provides these as raw SDK disclosures. You must evaluate how these categories map to specific Play Console form fields and determine your final "Collection & sharing" declarations based on your complete application binary.

### Purpose breakdown

1. **Advertising or marketing**: Data such as ad interactions and identifiers enable Google and demand sources to evaluate ad performance and serve contextual or personalised ads.
2. **Analytics**: Performance data and product interaction metrics help evaluate SDK performance, fill rates, and ad delivery metrics.
3. **Fraud prevention, security, and compliance**: IP addresses, diagnostic information, and app set IDs are used to detect invalid click activity, prevent bot traffic, and enforce platform security policies.

## Advertising ID (AD_ID) permission

The Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically merges the `com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID` permission into your application manifest. For apps targeting Android 13 (API level 33) or higher, this permission is required to access the Google Advertising ID.

### Opting out of AD_ID

If your application is child-directed or requires an explicit opt-out from advertising identifier collection, you can remove the permission in your `AndroidManifest.xml`:

```xml
<uses-permission
    android:name="com.google.android.gms.permission.AD_ID"
    tools:node="remove" />
```

Removing `AD_ID` prevents the SDK from accessing the device advertising ID. When `AD_ID` is removed, update your Data safety declaration accordingly. Note that non-identifier data categories—such as IP address, diagnostic information, and app product interactions—continue to be collected for ad delivery and fraud prevention.

### Age-restricted and child-directed treatment

Applications aimed at children or mixed audiences must configure ad request settings in addition to UMP consent options. `AdConfig` separates UMP consent flags from Google Mobile Ads request flags:

```kotlin
AdConfig(
    androidAppId = "ca-app-pub-...",
    iosAppId = "ca-app-pub-...",
    consentTagForUnderAgeOfConsent = true,                 // UMP consent flow configuration
    ageRestrictedTreatment = AgeRestrictedTreatment.Child, // GMA ad request configuration
)
```

Setting `ageRestrictedTreatment` to `Child` or `Teen` instructs the Google Mobile Ads SDK to serve appropriate non-personalised or restricted ad requests regardless of manifest permissions (see [Google's child-directed setting documentation](https://developers.google.com/admob/android/targeting#child-directed_setting)).

## Publisher evaluation checklist

Because Google Play Data safety declarations reflect the entire application binary rather than a single SDK, you must make the final decisions about your form answers. Verify the following before submitting your Play Console declaration:

- [ ] **Mediation adapters**: Have you checked the specific data safety disclosures for each mediation partner (e.g. Meta Audience Network, AppLovin, Unity Ads)? Additional adapters often collect extra device identifiers or user data.
- [ ] **Custom analytics and backend logging**: Does your application send ad revenue events (`onPaidEvent`) or diagnostic details to your own backend server or third-party analytics services?
- [ ] **Location services**: Does your app request precise location permissions (`ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION`)? While GMA derives coarse location from IP address, custom app logic requesting fine location requires separate Play form declarations.
- [ ] **Regional consent settings**: Are you using UMP consent forms to restrict data collection in applicable regions?
